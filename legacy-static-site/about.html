About BikerClinic

Born from a passion for bikes and frustration with unreliable mechanics — here's how BikerClinic became Bangalore's most trusted bike service platform.

🏍️ How We Started

Born in Bangalore, Built for Bikers

BikerClinic started in Nanjudaya Layout, Bangalore when our founder Kiran, a passionate Royal Enfield rider, faced a nightmare — a mechanic who charged ₹3,000 for a ₹500 job. That day, he decided to build something better.

We set out to create a transparent, hassle-free bike service experience where customers know exactly what they're paying for, and every service is done right the first time.

Today, BikerClinic has serviced 500+ bikes across Bangalore with a team of certified mechanics, and we're growing fast — one happy rider at a time.

📅 Our Journey

BikerClinic Milestones

2022
BikerClinic Founded
Kiran starts BikerClinic from his garage in Nanjudaya Layout with 2 mechanics and a mission for honest bike service.
2023
First 100 Bikes Serviced
Word of mouth spreads. BikerClinic crosses 100 bikes serviced and expands to cover all of South Bangalore.
2024
Online Booking Launched
BikerClinic goes digital with WhatsApp booking and website. Team grows to 6 certified mechanics covering all Bangalore.
2025
500+ Bikes & 4.9★ Rating
BikerClinic hits 500 bikes serviced, maintains a 4.9-star rating and launches the GoMechanic-style booking platform.
2027
Vision: 100 Service Points
Expanding to 100+ service points across Karnataka, becoming the go-to two-wheeler service platform in South India.
